Privacy Policy

MPC Pharma
Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MPC Pharma ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MPC Pharma mobile application (Android and iOS) and related web services (together, the "App"). The App is intended for authorised employees, partners, and customers of MPC Pharma.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account and identity information — mobile phone number used for OTP login, name, role, department, and other profile details provided by your organisation or entered in the App.
  • Location information — device location (including while the App is in use and, where permitted, in the background) to support delivery trips, route tracking, proximity checks, and location updates associated with deliveries.
  • Operational and business data — trip details, delivery documents, scan records, customer and firm information, ticket/complaint content, leave requests, signatures, comments, and status updates entered or generated through the App.
  • Camera and barcode data — images or scan results when you use barcode scanning features.
  • Audio recordings — voice messages you choose to attach to tickets or related records.
  • Files and attachments — documents, images, or other files you upload through the App.
  • Device and app information — app version, general device/OS information, and technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate users and provide access to authorised features
  • Manage pharmacy delivery operations, trips, scanning, and reporting
  • Support tickets, complaints, leave requests, and internal workflows
  • Display maps and location-related features
  • Send operational notifications where enabled
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and improve reliability of the App
  • Comply with applicable legal and business requirements

3. Legal basis and authorisation

The App is provided for business use by authorised users. By using the App, you confirm that you are permitted to access it and that information you submit is accurate and related to legitimate business activities.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host or support our systems (for example cloud hosting, SMS/OTP delivery, and map services), only as needed to operate the App
  • Other authorised users within your organisation when required for operations (for example managers, dispatch, or support teams)
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

5. Third-party services

The App may use third-party services such as Google Maps. Your use of those services may be subject to the third party's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the App, meet business and legal requirements, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary by data type and organisational policy.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and permissions

The App requests device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, and notifications) to provide specific features. You can manage many permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work if required permissions are denied.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and is intended for authorised business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
